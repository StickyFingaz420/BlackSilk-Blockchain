## E1. The hop lemma (L9) and the extended case tree

### E1.0 Setting

Take a zero-throwaway run that reaches m1, and its end window from stage 1's
L7:

| | Case A | Case B |
|---|---|---|
| e | 166 | 167 |
| t | 167 or 168 | 168 or 169 |

Here t is the P1 slot that is booked early. The **booker** is the P1-only uop
at P1@t. It was committed in a pass p ≤ e − 1, while cycle e was completely
free (L7). By the skip-cause lemma, the booker is either an IMUL_R (an IMUL_RR
macro-op created in a BUF_4444 slot 0-2; 483, cpp 381-382) or the IMUL_RR of
an IMUL_RCP. In both cases it skipped the free slot P1@e only through failed
register selections ("hops").

### E1.1 Bookings at the booker's selection. PROVEN (second reviewer)

**Statement.** When the booker's register selections run (between its trial
schedule at 766 and its commit at 818-819; cpp 703 and 762), every existing
port booking is at a cycle ≤ e − 1.

**Proof.**

1. No booking is ever removed. `port_busy` is zeroed only at 729 (cpp 656).
   `schedule_uop` writes only into empty slots (666-683; cpp 591-611).
2. So the bookings at the booker's selection are a subset of those at the
   start of pass e, minus the booker's own P1@t, which is not yet committed.
3. *Case B, start of pass 167* (L7 B):
   - cycle 167 is free;
   - P0/P5 at 168 and 169 are free (passes 168 and 169 take them);
   - of P1@168 and P1@169, exactly one is booked, by the booker;
   - no cycle ≥ 170 has a booking (stage-1 L2: a commit at ≥ 170 saturates,
     837-838; cpp 792-794).
4. *Case A, start of pass 166* (L7 A, L6 A):
   - cycle 166 is free;
   - P0/P5 at 167 and 168 are free (passes 167 and 168 take them);
   - of P1@167 and P1@168, exactly one is booked, by the booker;
   - cycle 169 is completely free (L6 A);
   - nothing is at ≥ 170 (L2).
5. In both cases the only booking at ≥ e is the booker's own. ∎

### E1.2 At most 3 registers not ready at cycle e. PROVEN (second reviewer)

**Statement.** At the booker's selections, at most 3 registers have
`latency > e`. Each of them was last written by the P1-only result uop of a
multiplication committed at a cycle in [e − 3, e − 1], one per P1 slot. A
write at e − 3 is possible only for MUL_R or IMUL_R_OP.

**Proof.**

1. A register's latency is set only at 827-832 (cpp 777-785), to
   `dep_cycle = commit + λ` (825; cpp 771), where λ is the result macro-op's
   latency (304-314; cpp 125-146):
   - λ = 1 for SUB_RR, XOR_RR, LEA_SIB, ROR_RI, ADD_RI and XOR_RI;
   - λ = 3 for IMUL_RR (IMUL_R; result_op 1 of IMUL_RCP, 353);
   - λ = 4 for MUL_R and IMUL_R_OP (result_op 1 of IMULH_R/ISMULH_R, 351-352).
   The initial latency is 0 (431-437; cpp 349).
2. By E1.1, every result-op commit so far (each books a port; MOV_RR is never
   a result op) is at c ≤ e − 1. A register whose last write
   has (c, λ) is not ready at e iff c + λ > e.
   - λ = 1 would need c ≥ e. Impossible.
   - So λ ∈ {3, 4} and c ≥ e − λ + 1 ≥ e − 3, with λ = 3 needing c ≥ e − 2.
3. These result macro-ops carry a P1-only uop: IMUL_RR, or MUL_R/IMUL_R_OP's
   uop 1. Distinct registers have distinct last-write commits, and these occupy
   distinct P1 slots (stage-1 L3) at cycles in [e − 3, e − 1]. At most three. ∎

### E1.3 Every booker fails a destination selection at cycle e. PROVEN (after the second review's correction)

**Statement.**

- The booker's trial schedule s satisfies e − 5 ≤ s ≤ e for an IMUL_R and
  e − 2 ≤ s ≤ e for an IMUL_RCP's IMUL_RR.
- Its last selection cycle `sc` satisfies sc ≥ e + 1.
- The tries at cycles s, …, sc − 1 all failed, and the try at e is a
  **destination** failure.
- No source failure happens at e or later.

**Proof.**

1. *s ≤ e.* This is the skip-cause lemma, steps 2-3. The trial returns the
   first free P1 at or after a start ≤ e (`schedule_uop`), and P1@e is free.
2. *Failed tries.* Each failed try raises `schedule_cycle` by 1 (777, 802;
   cpp 717, 741). At most 3 tries can fail in each loop (`forward` restarts at 773
   and 793; cpp 715, 739), since a 4th failure is a throwaway (781, 806; cpp 721, 744).
3. *sc ≥ e + 1.* The commit (818-819) scans P1 upward from the final
   `schedule_cycle` sc. If sc ≤ e, it would find P1@e free and commit at
   ≤ e < t, a contradiction. So sc ≥ e + 1.
4. *s ≥ e − 5 (IMUL_R), s ≥ e − 2 (RCP).* By step 2, sc − s ≤ 6, or ≤ 3 for
   an RCP, which has no source loop.
5. *Every cycle in [s, sc − 1] is a failed try, so e is one.* The source loop
   (772-790) runs before the destination loop (792-815).
   - An IMUL_R has `src_op = dst_op = 0` (343).
   - An IMUL_RCP's IMUL_RR has `src_op = −1` and `dst_op = 1` (353), so it has
     no source loop.
6. *The try at e is not a source failure.* A source failure at a cycle c' needs
   `latency > c'` for all 8 registers (606-608, 648-650; cpp 516-521, 332-334).
   - At c' = e that contradicts E1.2 (at most 3).
   - At c' > e, fewer registers are not ready (latencies are fixed during the
     selections).
   So the source phase ended before e, and the try at e is a destination
   failure. ∎

**Consequence (source hops):** an IMUL_R source hop can occur only at cycles
s … e − 1, with s ≥ e − 5, at most 3 of them. It needs all 8 registers not ready there. This is
the case-tree node "source hops" below.

### E1.4 L9: the tag requirement at cycle e. PROVEN (second reviewer)

**Statement.**

- *(T-IMUL_R)* If the booker is an IMUL_R, then at its destination try at e,
  at least 4 registers other than its source are ready and tagged
  `last_op_group = IMUL_R`. Each was last written by an IMUL_R committed at a
  cycle ≤ e − 3, and no ready register other than the source is untagged.
- *(T-RCP)* If the booker is an IMUL_RCP, at least 5 registers are ready and
  tagged `(IMUL_RCP, −1)`. Each was last written by an IMUL_RCP's IMUL_RR
  committed at ≤ e − 3, and **every** ready register is so tagged.

**Proof.**

1. *Destination rule* (580-586; cpp 510). Register i is available iff all of:
   - `latency ≤ cycle`;
   - `can_reuse || i ≠ src`;
   - `allow_chained_mul || group ≠ IMUL_R || last group ≠ IMUL_R`;
   - `(last group, last par) ≠ (group, par)`;
   - the IADD_RS/r5 rule.
2. With zero throwaways, `throw_away_count` = 0 (816 resets it, and nothing
   else raises it), so `allow_chained_mul` is false (797; cpp 739).
3. *IMUL_R.* Group IMUL_R, `can_reuse` false (520-525; cpp 431-436).
   - A ready register other than the source is unavailable iff its last group
     is IMUL_R. The par condition can fail only for an IMUL_R-tagged register.
   - By E1.2 at least 5 registers are ready. The source is among them: it was
     selected at a cycle ≤ e with `latency ≤` that cycle (606-607).
   - So at least 4 ready non-source registers carry the IMUL_R tag.
   - The tag is set only by an IMUL_R result commit (830, group from create
     523), with readiness c + 3 ≤ e.
4. *IMUL_RCP.* Group IMUL_RCP, par −1, `can_reuse` false, src = −1 (556-565;
   cpp 481-487).
   - Condition 2 holds for every i, condition 3 does not apply, and the r5
     rule is for IADD_RS only.
   - So a ready register is unavailable iff it is tagged (IMUL_RCP, −1).
   - Failure therefore means all ready registers, at least 5 by E1.2, carry
     that tag. ∎

**Relation to the plan's L9.** The plan stated "at least 4 / at least 5" for
a hop at any cycle c, conditional on "at most 3 registers not ready at c". E1.2
gives that condition at the cycle that matters, c = e, because of E1.1's empty
future. At a general cycle c < e, bookings at cycles c … e − 1 may exist, and
no bound is shown. That general form is **not needed** by the case tree: every
booker fails at e (E1.3). It stays unproven and is not claimed.

### E1.6 Corollary: no m1(L) for 5 ≤ L ≤ 8. PROVEN (second reviewer)

**Statement.** For a latency L with 5 ≤ L ≤ 8, no zero-throwaway run reaches
m1(L). Case A is also impossible for L = 9.

**Proof.**

1. Stage-1 L7 (which needs L ≥ 5) puts the window at e = L − 4 in case A and
   e = L − 3 in case B, with a booker in both. The skip-cause lemma, E1.3 and
   E1.4 hold with 170 replaced by L. Their only uses of 170 are L2's
   saturation bound and the window cycles.
2. E1.4 needs at least 4 IMUL_R-tagged registers, or at least 5 RCP-tagged
   ones, ready at e.
3. Each was last written by a distinct P1-only uop (stage-1 L3) committed at a
   cycle c with c + 3 ≤ e, so 0 ≤ c ≤ e − 3. At most e − 2 such slots exist.
4. So e ≥ 6 is necessary for an IMUL_R booker, and e ≥ 7 for an RCP booker.
5. Case B (e = L − 3) therefore needs L ≥ 9, and case A (e = L − 4) needs
   L ≥ 10. ∎

This agrees with stage 1's machine results for L = 5 and 6, and the E2 search
for L = 7 checks it independently (E2). Like stage 1's small-L results, **it
says nothing at L = 170**, where e − 2 = 164 or 165 slots are available.

The case tree below each window, with every node required:

```
booker ∈ {IMUL_R from a BUF_4444 slot 0-2 at pass p, IMUL_RCP's IMUL_RR at pass p}
 × t ∈ {e+1, e+2}
 × s ∈ {e−5, …, e} (IMUL_R) or {e−2, …, e} (RCP)    (E1.3)
 × source hops a ∈ {0, …, min(3, e−s)}, destination hops b ≤ 3, s + a + b ≥ e + 1
   (source hops: IMUL_R only; each at c' < e needs NR(c') = 8)
 → destination failure at e with T-IMUL_R or T-RCP     (E1.4, required)
 → if s < e: P1@s is free at the booker's trial, and N1 needs a later
   multiplication from a pass q with p ≤ q ≤ s to fill it
   (L1: no pass after s can fill cycle s)
```

**Where the descent stands (OPEN).** Every leaf needs the tag supply T-IMUL_R
(at least 4 live IMUL_R tags) or T-RCP (at least 5 live RCP tags, and no
ready register without that tag) at cycle e. Nothing in E1.1-E1.4 contradicts
these requirements locally:

- An IMUL_R destination may never carry the IMUL_R tag (allow_chained_mul is
  false), so every IMUL_R adds one IMUL_R-tagged register, and only a later
  write of another group removes it.
- So two BUF_4444 passes can supply 6 tags.
- Each IMUL_RCP adds one RCP tag likewise (its destination may not carry
  (IMUL_RCP, −1)).

The local argument therefore stops at the obstacle the run G research found:
a history that supplies the tags.

**What closing it would need** (none of these exists):

1. *A global bound on live tags under N1.* Show that a zero-throwaway history
   in which every P1 slot of cycles 0 … e − 1 holds a multiplication (stage-1
   L6), with the fetch rule `mul_count < cycle + 1` → BUF_4444 (409-410; cpp
   280-281), cannot have at least 4 IMUL_R-tagged registers ready, non-source,
   at cycle e together with NR(e) ≤ 3 and cycle e free. For the RCP case it
   must likewise rule out at least 5 live RCP tags with every ready register
   RCP-tagged. A candidate route is to count, per cycle, the writes that remove
   tags (every non-IMUL_R result writes some register, at least two per pass
   in the window shapes) against the tag-creating multiplications that N1
   allows. Not attempted.
2. Or a descent: show that the tag supply itself forces a lower window shape,
   with an induction down to decode cycle 0, where every register is untagged
   (431-437). The run G research's lead covered only one branch.
3. Or a finite exhaustive check of the tag-supply sub-history (the last few
   decode cycles before e, with the unknown history over-approximated by
   arbitrary tags). This is the run G window model, which did not close at
   s = 165. It would need the E1.1-E1.4 constraints as extra pruning, and a
   stated abstraction-soundness argument.

None of these is E1 work. They are E3 or beyond, not approved.
