## E3.1 The descent case tree

A zero-throwaway run that reaches m1 has, by stage-1 L7, an end window: case A
(e = 166, buffer shape 3310/493/follow-on/3310 at decode 166-169) or case B
(e = 167, follow-on/3310/493 at 167-169). In both, a P1 slot P1@t with
t ∈ {e+1, e+2} is booked early, by a **booker** committed in a pass p ≤ e − 1
while cycle e is free (E1). The booker is an IMUL_R (from a BUF_4444 slot 0-2,
483; cpp 381-382) or an IMUL_RCP's IMUL_RR (353; cpp 481-487).

```
m1 (zero throwaways)
└─ window W ∈ {A at e=166, B at e=167}                        (stage-1 L7, PROVEN)
   └─ booker b ∈ {IMUL_R, IMUL_RCP-IMUL_RR}, t ∈ {e+1, e+2}
      trial s ∈ [e−5, e] (IMUL_R) or [e−2, e] (RCP)           (E1.3, PROVEN)
      ├─ branch S  source hops: a ≤ 3 source failures at cycles in [s, e−1],
      │            each needs NR(c') = 8 (all registers not ready)  (E1.3)
      ├─ branch D  destination hop through cycle e, needing
      │            T-IMUL_R (≥4 ready non-source IMUL_R-tagged regs) or
      │            T-RCP (≥5 ready (IMUL_RCP,−1)-tagged regs)        (E1.4, PROVEN)
      └─ (if s < e) N1 fill: P1@s free at the trial ⇒ a multiplication from a
                    pass q ≤ s fills P1@s                            (stage-1 L1)
   descent step D(W): "window W at e needs a window W' at some e' < e in the
   closed set Σ, or a local contradiction." Σ must be closed and hold both
   roots; the base (decode ≤ 4, every register INVALID_GROUP, 431-437) has no
   tag, so no booker.
```

Closure = every node of every branch ends in (i) a local contradiction or
(ii) a strictly-lower window in Σ, with Σ finite and closed. E3 asked whether
D(W) holds.

## E3.2 Branch S (source hops): REFUTED as closable by "NR = 8 impossible"

A source hop at cycle c' needs all 8 registers not ready (`latency > c'` for
every register; 606-608, 648-650; cpp 516-521, 332-334). One might hope to
close branch S by showing NR(c') = 8 is unreachable for the real generator (the
run G record asserted, informally, that "all 8 registers unavailable for 3
cycles ... the real generator cannot give").

**That hope is REFUTED.** The `nrstat` measurement (E3.5) over 20,000 keys ×
8 programs at L = 170 found NR = 8 at a selection cycle **6,872,410** times,
and `select_source` failing outright (NR = 8 at the source trial, a real
source hop) **92,267** times. So NR = 8 and source hops are ordinary events in
the real generator, not forbidden. Branch S cannot be closed locally; it stays
**OPEN**.

(The measured hops are spread over whole programs, not the end window. The
measurement refutes a blanket impossibility; it does not show the hop is
reachable *at* e under the window's N1 constraints, which is the undischarged
obligation.)

## E3.3 Branch D (tag supply): T-IMUL_R satisfiable, T-RCP not observed

E1.4 (PROVEN) says a destination hop through e needs T-IMUL_R (≥ 4 ready
non-source registers tagged `last_op_group = IMUL_R`) for an IMUL_R booker, or
T-RCP (≥ 5 ready registers tagged `(IMUL_RCP, −1)`) for an IMUL_RCP booker. A
descent would need to show that supplying these tags forces a strictly-lower
window. The local obstacle: an IMUL_R destination may never itself carry the
IMUL_R tag (`allow_chained_mul` false with zero throwaways, 797; cpp 739), and
an IMUL_RCP destination may never carry `(IMUL_RCP, −1)`, so each such
multiplication *adds* one live tag that only a later different-group write
removes. Two BUF_4444 passes can therefore supply 6 IMUL_R tags locally,
without any lower window of the window shapes.

| 1 | 5,596,445 | 2,168,136 |
| 2 | 4,299,078 | 197,939 |

| 5 | 6,817 | 0 |
| 6 | 1,698 | 0 |

- **T-IMUL_R (≥ 4): satisfiable.** 174,106 real selections had ≥ 4 ready
  non-source IMUL_R-tagged registers (up to 7). So the IMUL_R-booker branch
  cannot be closed by "the tag supply is impossible." It stays **OPEN**.
- **T-RCP (≥ 5): never observed.** The most ever seen was 4, once, in
  20,000 keys × 8 programs. ≥ 5 did not occur. This is **evidence, not a
  proof**: it suggests the IMUL_RCP-booker branch might be closable
  analytically (an IMUL_RCP writes r once, its group/par bound the reuse), but
  E3 did not prove it. It stays **OPEN** (now with a concrete target: bound the
  number of simultaneously-live `(IMUL_RCP, −1)` tags at ≤ 4).

## E3.4 Why the descent does not close, and why brute force cannot discharge it

1. **No local contradiction.** E3.2 and E3.3 show both branch premises are
   satisfiable in the real generator (except the RCP sub-case, only
   unobserved). So D(W) cannot be met by (i) alone.
2. **No terminating descent found.** The only route is (ii): the tag/NR supply
   forces a lower window in Σ. The supply can be produced by instructions in the
   preceding ~5 decode cycles (E1.3: s ≥ e − 5) whose placement does not force a
   window of a shape already in Σ — the run G finding ("unknown history may
   supply 5 RCP (or 4 IMUL_R) tags, exactly the hop requirement"). E3 adds that
   the IMUL_R supply is not merely possible but common.
3. **Brute force is infeasible.** The sound register-free over-approximation's
   exhaustive reachability (stage 1 method (f)), which settled L ≤ 7, **does not
   finish even its register-free phase at L = 8** within 250 CPU-s (E3.5); its
   middle-decode levels grow super-exponentially. L = 170 is unreachable by this
   method by a wide margin. The run G window model (exact registers, unknown
   history from decode s) did not finish its first level from s = 165 and
   aborted at 6.1 GB. Neither fits E3's 6 GB / 40 CPU-h bounds. A key search is
   out of scope and has ≈ 0 expected yield (stage-1 estimate).

Because the premises are satisfiable (so no quick contradiction) **and** every
sound exhaustive discharger is infeasible, E3 was stopped without launching a
multi-hour search: an open-ended campaign is disallowed by the bounds and could
not close the descent regardless. CPU actually spent: about 0.2 CPU-hours
(E3.5). This is a deliberate early stop, not budget exhaustion; the owner may
fund a different route (below).

## E3.7 GO/NO-GO report

   as a closure.
2. **Unresolved premise/branch.** Both: branch S (a source hop at e needs
   NR = 8, which is common) and branch D (an IMUL_R destination hop needs ≥ 4
   live IMUL_R tags, which is common). The descent step D(W) — "the supply

   forcing a strictly-lower window of a shape in Σ, so the descent does not
   terminate; and every sound exhaustive discharger is infeasible within the

   model aborted at 6.1 GB from s = 165). No terminating analytic descent and no
   feasible exhaustive check exists today.

   MB. A deliberate early stop: the premises are satisfiable and brute force is
   infeasible, so a multi-hour search was not launched (open-ended campaigns are
   disallowed and would not close it).
5. **Evidence and limits.** Evidence: the `nrstat` histograms (sound
   instrumentation of the validated real generator) and the L = 8 register-free

   reachability at e; the register-free infeasibility is a timing observation,
