### 1.2 Security-critical classes for the supply audit

- **SC1, false conservation (most severe):** any mutant under which the audit reports
  COMPLETE or no alarm, or exit 0 with `--expect-complete`, while (a) the wallets hold
  more than `generated` (inflation, or a kept spent output), (b) `px_pool` or ΣPX is
  miscounted, or (c) a chain check failed. Members: `complete`, `alarm` and `exit_code`;
  655-669; 630-652 (totals and dedup); 424-433 (pool); `main`/`run` exit codes.
- **SC2, chain or sync integrity:** the linkage, emission, coinbase, genesis, network and
  sync-state checks, the reorg check (643-644), and `Pinned`, which lets wallets see past
  H or submit transactions (the read-only guarantee). A survivor here can compare
  holdings and chain at different heights or forks.
- **SC3, report and evidence:** `text`, `amt`, the `summarize` subtotals (immature,
  locked, reserved; per-wallet only, since the totals are recomputed in `audit`),
  `total_difference`, duplicate counts. Operator-facing; the trial evidence quotes them.
- **SC4, CLI and wallet-file safety:** password files, `file_kdf` (`--save` keeps the
  file's KDF parameters), argument checks.

Rule: an SC1 or SC2 survivor is **never exempted on a "low impact" argument**. It is
killed, or shown equivalent from the code with a second reviewer. SC3 and SC4 survivors
are killed where a cheap unit test can do it (expected: all), and otherwise exempted with
a code-grounded argument, as E33 and E73 were.

### 2.2 (b) The descent proof: case tree, lemmas, mechanical checks

**Case tree** (roots from L6 and L7; every node must be closed):

```
m1 (zero throwaways)
├─ A    3310 at 169 (current IMULH_R/ISMULH_R), M(169)=169, cycle 169 free
├─ B-d  default 493 at 169, M(169)=170, P0+P5 free at 169      [UNCHECKED in the research]
└─ B-r  IMUL_RCP follow-on 493 at 169, M(169)=170, P0+P5 free   [the one descending case seen]
each root → "who booked P1 at e−1, and when":
   booker ∈ {IMUL_R (only from 4444 slots 0-2, 483) [the 4444 IMUL_R hop branch: UNCHECKED],
             IMULH_R/ISMULH_R (SLOT_3L, P1+P5 same cycle),
             IMUL_RCP IMUL_RR (slot 10 of 3310 only, 488; dependent, 353)}
   × booked at its own decode cycle | after a hop of k = 1..3 cycles (L9 tag requirement)
   × buffer at that decode cycle ∈ {484, 7333, 3733, 493, 4444, 3310}
→ leaf: (i) local contradiction (ports, N1/N4, L5-type saturation), or
        (ii) shape S' at a lower decode cycle e' < e (descend), or
        (iii) a tag requirement T not explained by any lower hop  ⇒ the descent FAILS on this branch
```

**Lemma per node:** `D(S, e)`: a zero-throwaway run with shape S at decode e implies
(i) or (ii), with the induction hypothesis that every shape in the closed set Σ is
unreachable below e. Base: at decode ≤ 4, check exhaustively with the existing tag model
(sound and validated; 9, 2,234, 60,835 and 33,530 states at decode boundaries 0-3 [V,
`runs/tsearch.txt`]). Every register starts `INVALID_GROUP` (431-437), so no tag
requirement holds there. The proof holds when Σ is closed under D and contains every
root.

**Mechanical verification per node** (a scratch crate outside the repo, as `G3/rx742/`):

1. **Branch enumerator:** an exhaustive check of the local window [e − W, e + 4] (W ≤ 6
   decode cycles; 4 is `LOOK_FORWARD_CYCLES`, 657). Exact ports, buffers, the current
   instruction and macro-op index, and `L = M − d`. Registers are abstracted as
   (ready-relative-to-window, tag ∈ {ImulR, ImulRcp, Mulh(par), other, INVALID,
   unknown}). "Unknown" over-approximates, as in `m742_win.rs`. N1 and N4 act as
   constraints. The output per node is a list of lower shapes (feeding Σ), or a
   contradiction, or a failure witness for (iii).
2. **The missing ingredient** (why the window model failed): unknown history can supply
   4 or 5 tagged registers. The window check must therefore discharge each tag
   requirement T by showing that its tagged writes force a lower hop or shape in Σ. If
   T can be supplied by ordinary non-hop multiplications left unwritten, the branch is
   (iii) and the descent does not close there. Record that as a result, not a failure of
   effort.
3. **Soundness validation** against the real generator (`generate()` instrumented, the
   research's logged-generator pattern) on **≥ 10,000 keys (≥ 80,000 programs)**, with
   "test key 000" included. Every real transition must be contained in the abstract
   relation, and the abstraction of every real window state must be contained in an
   abstract state. Precedent: `tval-12000` checked 12,001 keys in 59.5 s [V]. Random keys
   almost never reach the end windows (272 zero-throwaway programs in 32M, latest exit at
   decode 168 [V]), so also validate on the tilted sampler's 1.5M decode-169 states
   (`rxreach/tilt-20M.txt` [V]). As a negative control, seed one deliberate rule error
   into each model; validation must fail.
4. **Closure check:** a script over the enumerator outputs confirms that Σ is closed and
   every node is (i) or (ii). It is published with commands, seeds, binary hashes and
   logs.

**Go/no-go after the first pass:** enumerate the B-r branch (the lead) and the two
unchecked branches (B-d at e − 1, the 4444 IMUL_R hop) to depth 3. If any reaches (iii),
stop the descent route and report it to the owner (decision point D2).

### 2.3 (c) Alternatives and expected yield

- **Key search (brute force or tilted):** expected yield for RESOLVED-KILLED ≈ 0.
  - The per-program probability of zero throwaways is estimated at about 1.2 × 10⁻²⁶
    (`run-0-4M.txt` [V the logged figure; I its method]).
  - The importance-sampling estimates near decode 169 to 170 are about 10⁻²⁷ to 10⁻²⁸
    (`tilt-20M.txt` [V figures]).
  - At an optimistic 10⁷ programs/s, that is no hit in any feasible budget.
- **Larger tag-guided beams** (width 10⁴ to 10⁵, the m742_full model): these search
  draw sequences, not keys. Converting a witness into a key needs a Blake2b preimage of a
  chained state (12-31), which is infeasible.
  - A model witness would therefore prove the mutant **non-equivalent but unkillable**
    under the criteria. That decides the equivalence route negatively; the item stays
    OPEN (decision D2).
  - Cost: fbeam width 1,000 to decode 169 took 484 s [V]; width 10⁵ is about 13-15
    CPU-h and 2-6 GB [I].
- **SAT/SMT on the window constraints:** the same CNF per descent node: one-hot ports
  per (cycle, port), buffer per decode cycle, slot type, macro-op cycle bounded to the
  window, register latency classes and tags, and N1/N4.
  - UNSAT discharges the node's lemma under the same abstraction-soundness validation
    (step 3).
  - Tooling: a pure-Rust solver in a scratch crate outside the repo (varisat, splr or
    batsat — [I] availability, licence and proof-certificate support not checked). No
    project dependency.
  - Cross-check: every UNSAT result is reproduced by the enumerator on small W, or by a
    second solver (a DRAT-checked certificate if available). Every real validation
    window, with the asserted contradiction removed, must be SAT.
  - Yield: helps (b); never yields a key.
- **Not in scope without the owner:** making `generate()` take an injectable draw source
  (a production refactor) would allow a white-box test with a crafted stream. That would
  kill only under a changed criterion (not a real key). Decision point D3.

### 2.5 (e) Pass/fail criteria

- **RESOLVED-EQUIVALENT** only with a complete written proof covering m1 for runs with
  and without throwaways: R0, R1 and L0-L9, every branch of the case tree closed, and
  every machine check reproducible and validated per §2.2 step 3. A second reviewer must
  re-verify it independently, line by line, against the code at the then-current commit.
  It then becomes an exemption entry (next free E number), run G open item (a) and the
  STATUS mutation row are updated, and the scratch artefacts are listed as internal
  records.
- **RESOLVED-KILLED** only with a real key (and input) such that all of the following
  hold:
  - the mutant's hash differs;
  - the original hash equals the reference: `C:/bszkeval/rx-ref/kat-corpus-bs.exe`
    (tevador/RandomX v1.2.3, BlackSilk salt) in interp, jit and auto modes;
  - the line is added to `randomx/tests/data/randomx-reference-light-v1.txt` and

  - a kill record exists: cargo-mutants 27.1.0, `--profile mutants --cap-lints true
    --jobs 1`, an anchored `--re` for 742:24, oracle `--test=equivalence`, at the final
    commit (the runG3k783 pattern).
- **Otherwise OPEN.** A model witness without a key, a partial descent, or a budget
  exhausted all leave it OPEN, reported as such. Nothing here calls it accepted.

### 2.6 (f) Owner decision points

- **D1 budget cap** for (a) plus (b), for example 10 agent-days and 100 CPU-h, after
  which the item is reported OPEN with the evidence. Whether to then accept the residual
  is the owner's decision.
- **D2** if the descent fails on a branch (iii), or a beam finds a model witness:
  non-equivalent in principle, with no key findable. Choose: accept as a residual, or
  fund more work.
- **D3** whether to allow a production refactor (an injectable draw source) or a changed
  kill criterion (a crafted stream instead of a real key). Neither is ours to choose.
- **D4** at the freeze: if still OPEN, accept the residual explicitly (the shipped bound
  equals the reference's, `superscalar.cpp:678` per the README) or hold the gate.
