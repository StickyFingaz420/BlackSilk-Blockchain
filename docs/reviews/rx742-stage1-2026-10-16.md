### L0: cycle invariant. PROVEN

**Statement.** With zero throwaways, `cycle == decode_cycle` at every top of the
outer loop.

**Proof.**

1. `cycle` changes only at four places:
   - 778 and 803, by +1 per failed selection (cpp 718, 742);
   - 840, back to `top_cycle`, which 750 sets at the top of each slot (cpp 795,
     688);
   - 847, by +1, together with `decode_cycle` at 848 (cpp 803, 678).
2. The only ways out of a slot iteration that skip 840 are the throwaway
   `continue`s (785, 810) and the breaks (754, 769, 788, 813, 822).
3. Every break leaves the inner loop.
4. Within a slot, the increments at 778 and 803 come before 840. 840 then resets
   `cycle` to the value it had at the top of that slot.
5. By induction over the slots of the pass, `cycle` at the top of every slot of
   pass d equals its value at the start of pass d. So does `cycle` at 847,
   unless a break occurred after some hops.
6. Breaks at 754 and 769 come before any hop of their slot.
7. Breaks at 788 and 813 need 256 throwaways, so they cannot occur.
8. The break at 822 follows the hops of its slot and skips 840. But it sets
   `ports_saturated`, so the loop ends and m1 is not reached. Only runs that
   reach m1 matter.
9. At 847 and 848 both values grow by 1. With `cycle = decode_cycle = 0`
   initially (735, 741), the invariant holds at every top of the loop that a
   run reaching m1 passes through. ∎

### L1: commits are never below the decode cycle. PROVEN

**Statement.** Every macro-op committed in pass d sits at a cycle ≥ d. A cycle
below d never gains a port booking in pass d or later.

**Proof.**

1. By L0, `cycle = d` at the start of pass d. Within pass d, at the top of every
   slot `cycle ≥ d`, since 840 restores `top_cycle ≥ d` (L0, step 5).
2. The trial schedule (766; cpp 703) starts at `cycle` and never returns a
   smaller value:
   - `schedule_mop` (689-719; cpp 616-650) only raises its start, through `max`
     at 697;
   - an eliminated op returns its start (699-701);
   - `schedule_uop` (662-687; cpp 586-613) scans upward from the start;
   - the two-uop loop (706-717) starts at `cycle` and only increases it.
3. Hops (777, 802) raise `schedule_cycle`.
4. The commit (818-819; cpp 762) starts from `schedule_cycle` and, by the same
   monotonicity, returns at least that. So commits are ≥ d.
5. `schedule_uop` writes only the slot it returns (668, 674, 680), so all
   bookings of pass d are at cycles ≥ d.
6. Later passes have larger d. ∎

### L3: multiplications and P1 (corrected wording). PROVEN

**Statement.** Every multiplication instruction (IMUL_R, IMULH_R, ISMULH_R,
IMUL_RCP; `is_multiplication` 721-726, cpp 44-46) commits **at least one P1-only
uop**. The P1-only uops of different instructions occupy distinct P1 slots.
Non-multiplications commit no P1-only uop, but their P015 and P01 uops can still
land on P1. An IMUL_RCP's MOV_RI64 (P015) can also land on P1, so "exactly one
P1 uop" (plan L3) is false. "At least one P1-only uop" is the correct form.

**Proof.**

1. The macro-op lists (340-353; cpp 213-240):
   - IMUL_R is [IMUL_RR], with IMUL_RR = P1 (310).
   - IMULH_R is [MOV_RR, MUL_R, MOV_RR] and ISMULH_R is
     [MOV_RR, IMUL_R_OP, MOV_RR]. MUL_R and IMUL_R_OP have uops P1 and P5, both
     committed in the same cycle (706-714; cpp 635-646).
   - IMUL_RCP is [MOV_RI64 (P015), IMUL_RR dependent].
2. An instruction is pushed (842-845) only after all its macro-ops are
   committed, so each pushed multiplication has committed its P1-only uop.
3. `schedule_uop` writes a slot only when it is 0 (666-683), so no two uops
   share a slot.
4. Non-multiplications use SUB_RR, XOR_RR, ADD_RI and XOR_RI (P015), LEA_SIB
   (P01) and ROR_RI (P05). None of these is P1-only.
5. P015 and P01 uops try P5, then P0, then P1 (663-683), so they can take P1. ∎

### L6 (= R2): the two cases at pass 169. PROVEN

following holds.

## 3. Small-L exhaustive search

### 3.1 The model

- **Location and version:** the scratch crate `C:/bszkeval/rx742-scratch`
  (outside the repository; its own local git repository). Commits:
  - `7280ed1`: the model, validation, plain search;
  - `e5b8665`: adds (c'), the depth-first tail, the positive control and the
    pass-cost check;
  - `c70a222`: adds the register-free over-approximation and the guided
    search;
  - `0c4feb2`: adds `guided-dfs`.

  `model.rs` is unchanged between `e5b8665` and `0c4feb2` except for the
  `free_hops` branches, which are inert when the flag is off.
  - Built with rustc 1.98.1, `cargo build --release --offline`, target dir
    `C:/bszkeval/t-rx742`.
  - Binaries `rx742-model.exe`: `e5b8665` SHA-256 `b74a9776…95e3c874`;
    `0c4feb2` SHA-256 `89836777…1e61844d`.
  - Run logs are in `runs/` there (not committed).
  - Dependencies: `blake2 =0.10.6` (Blake2b-512 for the generator),
    `rustc-hash`, `windows-sys` (the resource guard).
  - It does **not** depend on `blacksilk-randomx`. `generate()` is `pub(crate)`
    and the crate exposes no program API, so a path dependency could not reach
    the programs without changing `randomx/`, which is forbidden here.
- **Independence:**
  - `src/model.rs` is written from cpp (each function cites its cpp lines),
    not from `superscalar.rs`.
  - Every draw goes through a `Chooser`: the real Blake2Generator
    (`blake2_generator.cpp` 34-62), or a script of decision outcomes.
  - The latency L is a run-time parameter: `CYCLE_MAP_SIZE = L + 4` and the
    size limit 3L + 2 (`common.hpp` 81).
- **Search space:** the decisions are enumerated, not byte streams.
  - Buffer choice (2 or 4 outcomes) and slot choice (2 or 4).
  - Register choice: all n candidates.
  - The IMULH/ISMULH par: "fresh" or equal to any par already held by a
    register of the same group. This includes the 2⁻³² collisions, so the
    search is a superset of the real outcomes.
  - Immediates that never influence scheduling (IADD_RS mod, IROR_C, C7/C8/C9,
    IMUL_RCP divisors) are not branched.
- **Pruning and reductions** (each sound for "reach m1(L) with zero
  throwaways"):
  - (a) a throwaway or a saturation cuts the branch (R1, L2);
  - (b) dead-P1: at least 2 P1 slots in [0, L) that can never hold a P1-only
    uop cut the branch. Either the slot is taken by a non-multiplication uop,
    or it is empty below the current decode cycle (L1). m1 needs
    M ≥ L − 1 at some time (L6: case A has M(L−1) = L−1, case B M(L−1) = L),
    and L4 bounds M by the P1-only slots;
  - (c) capacity: the remaining passes need at least g(r) free port slots in
    [d, L), by a 2/3 recurrence. Every pass fills its buffer, and only MOV_RR
    is port-free (the comment at `min_port_uses` in `src/main.rs` gives the
    proof). Checked after every commit (with the minimum over both starting
    shapes) and at pass ends;
  - (c') two-resource capacity: the same recurrence, counted separately for
    P1-only uops (they need free P1 slots) and for other port-using uops. The
    other uops need free P0/P5 slots, plus at most 1 − dead P1 slots, since a
    non-multiplication on P1 is a dead slot. The per-buffer minima are in the
    comment at `cap_table` in `src/main.rs`. The rows are a superset: every
    buffer is allowed whatever M is. The table was also checked empirically
    (§3.2);
  - (d) deduplication at pass boundaries on a canonical state:
    - ports below d keep only "P1 holds a P1-only uop";
    - ports at ≥ L, latencies ≥ L and the tags of registers that can no
      longer be selected are dropped. A selection or schedule at a cycle ≥ L
      always ends in saturation;
    - latency and `dep_cycle` are clamped to [d, L];
    - the fields of a finished current instruction are dropped (create resets
      or overwrites them);
    - IMULH pars are renumbered;
    - registers are renamed by a permutation fixing r5;
  - (e) orbit reduction: only one candidate per class of registers swappable
    by an automorphism of the state (`reg_class` in `src/model.rs`).
- **(f) Register-free over-approximation and the guided exact search**
  (`guided`, `guided-dfs`):
  - In the register-free model (`State::free_hops`), every source or
    destination selection succeeds after a freely chosen 0 to 3 hops, and
    registers are never read or written.
  - It contains every real zero-throwaway behaviour: a real selection
    succeeds after some k ≤ 3 failed tries (4 is a throwaway), and registers
    influence nothing but selections. The IADD_RS r5 rule only fixes which
    register is chosen.
  - The register-free system is searched exhaustively first, with every edge
    kept, and its states that can reach m1(L) are computed backwards.
  - The exact search then cuts every exact state whose register-free image
    (`project`: registers reset, a pending instruction's src/dst → r0, the
    IMULH par "fresh") is not among them. Every image is also asserted to be
    a reachable register-free state, which checks the projection.
  - `guided-dfs` explores the exact states depth-first, with a capped
    per-level memo of exact states already shown not to reach m1.
- **Depth-first tail (`tail=k`):** the last k + 1 passes are explored
  depth-first, without storing or deduplicating states. This saves memory and
  changes nothing else.
- **Status of the search itself:** the second reviewer found the pruning rules
  (a)-(f) sound (§5). Rules (b) and (c) rely on L1, L3, L4 and L6, which are
  now PROVEN.

### 3.4 Interpretation

- m1(L) is unreachable for L = 4, 5, 6 and undecided for L = 7. These results
  **support** an L-generic proof only weakly: L0–L6 hold for every L ≥ 2,
  L7 for L ≥ 5, and the windows of L7 sit at e = L−4 and e = L−3.
- It **proves nothing at L = 170.** For small L the windows collide with the
  fixed opening:
  - pass 0 is always BUF_4444 (M(0) = 0 < 1), which books P1@0, P1@1 and P1@2
    with no possible hop (at each selection at least 5 registers are ready and not IMUL_R-tagged);
  - case A needs a BUF_3310 at e = L−4 with a pending H, and case B one at
    e = L−3, both with cycle e free.
  So L = 4 to 6 close through the opening: the only register-free paths to
  m1 need a hop in pass 0, which the register rules forbid. They do not close
  through the descent that L = 170 needs. L = 7 is the first L at which the
  opening does not decide it. Reachability at L = 170 depends on the long middle of the
  program, where the hop requirements of §2 (L7, the skip-cause lemma) have to
  be met.
